EPF After Death — Form 20, Pension (Form 10D) & EDLI (Form 5IF)

A deceased EPF member's family can claim the PF balance, a monthly pension and an insurance amount — each on a different form. None of these needs a succession certificate: they are claimed through the EPFO on the member's nomination or heirship.

Form 20 — final settlement of the PF balance

Form 20 is for the final EPF settlement by the nominee or the legal heir of a deceased member. It is submitted via the last employer to the EPFO (also online via EPFO death-claim filing). The death certificate and cancelled cheque / bank details are required.

Form 10D — monthly pension

Form 10D claims the monthly pension under the Employees' Pension Scheme — widow / widower pension, children pension and orphan pension.

Form 10C — withdrawal benefit

Form 10C is for the withdrawal benefit where the member died after age 58 with less than 10 years of service.

Form 5IF — EDLI insurance

Form 5IF claims the Employees' Deposit Linked Insurance (EDLI) where the member died while in service. EDLI is automatic for EPF members and is funded by the employer (0.5% of wages, capped at ₹75 a month) — there is no separate enrolment and no employee contribution. The maximum benefit is ₹7 lakh (35 times the average monthly wage of the last 12 months, wage capped at ₹15,000, plus a bonus of up to ₹1.75 lakh linked to the average PF balance). The minimum is ₹2.5 lakh (12+ months' service); since the 18 July 2025 amendment, the minimum is ₹50,000 where the average PF balance is below ₹50,000 or the member died before completing a year.

Which certificate do you need for this?

EPF claims are made on the EPFO's own forms (20 / 10D / 10C / 5IF) through the nominee or the legal heir — a succession certificate is not the route for PF money. If the EPFO asks for proof of heirship, a legal-heir certificate is the usual document.

Frequently asked questions

Which form claims the PF balance of a deceased member?
Form 20 — final settlement by the nominee or the legal heir, submitted via the last employer to the EPFO (also online). The death certificate and bank details are required.
What is EDLI and how much does it pay?
EDLI (Employees' Deposit Linked Insurance) is automatic life cover for EPF members who die while in service, claimed on Form 5IF. The maximum is ₹7 lakh; the minimum is ₹2.5 lakh (12+ months' service), or ₹50,000 after the 18 July 2025 amendment where the average PF balance is below ₹50,000.
Does the family get a monthly pension?
Yes, on Form 10D — widow / widower pension, children pension and orphan pension under the Employees' Pension Scheme.
Do I need a succession certificate for EPF?
No. EPF claims are made on the EPFO's own forms through the nominee or the legal heir. A legal-heir certificate is the usual proof of heirship if the EPFO asks for one.
