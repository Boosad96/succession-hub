Legal Heir Certificate in Telangana — MRO & MeeSeva (Single Source)

In Telangana the legal-heir certificate is issued by the Mandal Revenue Officer (MRO), through MeeSeva Telangana. In Hyderabad it is processed as a Family Membership Certificate.

Issuing authorityMandal Revenue Officer (MRO)
PortalMeeSeva Telangana
FeeNot verified — confirm on the portal
TimelineNot verified — confirm on the portal

How to apply

Apply through MeeSeva Telangana (meeseva.telangana.gov.in) or at the MRO's office.

What is not shown — and why

Only one source could be found for this route, so the fee and timeline are not published. The timeline is also in conflict (15 working days under the Telangana Right to Services Act per one guide; 7–14 working days per another). The MeeSeva Telangana portal link could not be opened in our checks — confirm the link works before relying on it.

Documents usually asked for

  • Death certificate of the deceased (mandatory)
  • Identity proof of the applicant and all heirs
  • Address proof
  • Ration card / family register showing all family members
  • Proof of relationship to the deceased
  • Affidavit / joint affidavit from all heirs (where the state asks for one)

Frequently asked questions

Who issues the legal-heir certificate in Telangana?
The Mandal Revenue Officer (MRO). In Hyderabad it is processed as a Family Membership Certificate via MeeSeva.
