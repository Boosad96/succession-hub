Legal Heir Certificate in Andhra Pradesh — MRO & MeeSeva (Single Source)

In Andhra Pradesh the legal-heir certificate is issued by the Mandal Revenue Officer (MRO) of the mandal where the deceased resided, through MeeSeva AP.

Issuing authorityMandal Revenue Officer (MRO)
PortalMeeSeva AP
FeeNot verified — confirm on the portal
TimelineNot verified — confirm on the portal

How to apply

Apply through MeeSeva AP (meeseva.ap.gov.in) or at the MRO's office.

What is not shown — and why

Only one source could be found for this route, so the fee and timeline are not published. The MeeSeva AP portal link could not be opened in our checks (it is confirmed by multiple guides but was not directly reachable) — confirm the link works before relying on it.

Documents usually asked for

  • Death certificate of the deceased (mandatory)
  • Identity proof of the applicant and all heirs
  • Address proof
  • Ration card / family register showing all family members
  • Proof of relationship to the deceased
  • Affidavit / joint affidavit from all heirs (where the state asks for one)
