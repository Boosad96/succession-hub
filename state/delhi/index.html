Surviving Member Certificate (SMC) in Delhi — SDM, e-District, No Fee, 14 Days

Delhi does not issue a document called a 'legal-heir certificate'. What it issues is a Surviving Member Certificate (SMC) from the Sub-Divisional Magistrate (SDM), Revenue Department, Government of NCT of Delhi — and that is what Delhi offices and banks mean when they ask for a legal-heir certificate.

Issuing authoritySub-Divisional Magistrate (SDM), Revenue Dept, NCT of Delhi
PortalDelhi e-District
FeeNo fee
Timeline14 days

How to apply

Apply on Delhi e-District (edistrict.delhigovt.nic.in) or at the SDM's office.

Fee and timeline

No fee is charged. The certificate is issued in 14 days / 14 working days.

Documents usually asked for

  • Death certificate of the deceased (mandatory)
  • Identity proof of the applicant and all heirs
  • Address proof
  • Ration card / family register showing all family members
  • Proof of relationship to the deceased
  • Affidavit / joint affidavit from all heirs (where the state asks for one)

Frequently asked questions

Does Delhi issue a legal-heir certificate?
Not by that name. Delhi issues a Surviving Member Certificate (SMC) through the SDM, Revenue Department.
What does it cost?
No fee.
How long does it take?
14 days / 14 working days.
