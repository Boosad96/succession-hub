Surviving Member Certificate (SMC) in Delhi — SDM, e-District, No Fee, 14 Days

Delhi does not issue a document called a 'legal-heir certificate'. What it issues is a Surviving Member Certificate (SMC) from the Sub-Divisional Magistrate (SDM), Revenue Department, Government of NCT of Delhi — and that is what Delhi offices and banks mean when they ask for a legal-heir certificate.

Issuing authoritySub-Divisional Magistrate (SDM), Revenue Dept, NCT of Delhi
PortalDelhi e-District
FeeNo fee
Timeline14 days

How to apply

Apply online on Delhi e-District (edistrict.delhigovt.nic.in) or in person at the SDM's office. The certificate is issued by the Sub-Divisional Magistrate (SDM), Revenue Department, Government of NCT of Delhi.

What the SMC is for

The SMC lists the surviving members of the deceased. It identifies the heirs — Delhi offices and banks ask for it when they need proof of who the surviving family members are. It does not by itself authorise collecting bank deposits or shares: for that, banks usually ask for a succession certificate, or accept a nomination or indemnity depending on the amount and the bank's rules.

SMC or succession certificate — which one?

Delhi has two routes. The SMC from the SDM proves who the surviving members are. A succession certificate is a court order from the District Court under Sections 370–390 of the Indian Succession Act, 1925, for collecting debts and securities such as bank deposits and shares. The court fee for a succession certificate in Delhi is 2.5% of the value of the debt or security in the certificate (Court Fees Act 1870 as amended for Delhi, Schedule I Article 12), and 4% on an extension under Section 376. No cap appears in the Article 12 text — one guide claims a Rs 3,00,000 cap, but that figure is not in the Act text, so confirm the fee with the District Court before filing. A succession certificate does not by itself transfer title to immovable property.

Fee and timeline

No fee is charged for the SMC. The certificate is issued in 14 days / 14 working days.

Documents usually asked for

  • Death certificate of the deceased (mandatory)
  • Identity proof of the applicant and all heirs
  • Address proof
  • Ration card / family register showing all family members
  • Proof of relationship to the deceased
  • Affidavit / joint affidavit from all heirs (where the state asks for one)

Frequently asked questions

Does Delhi issue a legal-heir certificate?
Not by that name. Delhi issues a Surviving Member Certificate (SMC) through the SDM, Revenue Department.
What does it cost?
No fee.
How long does it take?
14 days / 14 working days.
What is the SMC used for?
It proves who the surviving members of the deceased are, for Delhi offices and banks that ask for a legal-heir certificate. It identifies the heirs; it does not by itself authorise collecting bank deposits or shares.
Do I need an SMC or a succession certificate?
An SMC if an office or bank asks for proof of the surviving members. A succession certificate from the District Court if you need to collect a debt or security, such as a bank deposit or shares.
How much is the court fee for a succession certificate in Delhi?
2.5% of the value of the debt or security in the certificate (4% on an extension under Section 376). No cap appears in the Article 12 text, so confirm the fee with the District Court.
Can I apply for the SMC online?
Yes — on Delhi e-District (edistrict.delhigovt.nic.in), or in person at the SDM's office.
