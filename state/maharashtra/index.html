Legal Heir Certificate in Maharashtra — Why There Isn't One, and the Heirship Certificate

Maharashtra has no notified 'legal-heir certificate' service — not in Mumbai and not anywhere else in the state. The equivalent is an heirship certificate under Bombay Regulation VIII of 1827, applied for in the civil court.

No legal-heir certificate is issued in Maharashtra. Maharashtra has no notified 'legal-heir certificate' service. The equivalent is an heirship certificate under Bombay Regulation VIII of 1827, applied for in the civil court (City Civil Court / Bombay High Court original side, by value). The Maharashtra Court-Fees Act Sch.I Art.12 charges it at the probate rate on the property certified. For debts and securities, use a succession certificate from the District Court instead.

The heirship certificate route

Apply in the civil court (City Civil Court / Bombay High Court original side, by value) for an heirship certificate under Bombay Regulation VIII of 1827. The Maharashtra Court-Fees Act Sch.I Art.12 charges it at the probate rate on the property certified.

For debts and securities

Use a succession certificate from the District Court instead — that is the route for bank deposits, shares, PF and insurance.

Documents usually asked for

  • Death certificate of the deceased (mandatory)
  • Identity proof of the applicant and all heirs
  • Address proof
  • Ration card / family register showing all family members
  • Proof of relationship to the deceased
  • Affidavit / joint affidavit from all heirs (where the state asks for one)

Frequently asked questions

Can I get a legal-heir certificate in Maharashtra?
No — Maharashtra has no notified legal-heir certificate service.
What is the alternative?
An heirship certificate under Bombay Regulation VIII of 1827, from the civil court. For debts and securities, a succession certificate from the District Court.
