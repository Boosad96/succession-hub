Legal Heir Certificate in Tamil Nadu (Varisu) — Portal, Documents, Fee & Timeline

In Tamil Nadu the legal-heir certificate is called the Varisu certificate. It is issued by the Tahsildar (Taluk office) after a Village Administrative Officer / Revenue Inspector enquiry, through the TN e-Sevai portal.

Issuing authorityTahsildar (Taluk office)
PortalTN e-Sevai
Fee₹60 (e-Sevai service charge)
Timeline15–30 days

How to apply

Apply on TN e-Sevai (service code REV-114) or at the Taluk office. The application must include ALL the legal heirs — leaving one out can cause rejection.

Fee and timeline

The e-Sevai service charge is ₹60. The certificate is typically issued in 15–30 days (official guidance says about 2 weeks in some guides).

Documents usually asked for

  • Death certificate of the deceased (mandatory)
  • Identity proof of the applicant and all heirs
  • Address proof
  • Ration card / family register showing all family members
  • Proof of relationship to the deceased
  • Affidavit / joint affidavit from all heirs (where the state asks for one)

Frequently asked questions

What is the Varisu certificate?
The Varisu certificate is Tamil Nadu's legal-heir certificate. It identifies who the legal heirs of a deceased person are, and is used for government pension, gratuity and dues.
Who issues it?
The Tahsildar (Taluk office), after a VAO / Revenue Inspector enquiry.
What does it cost and how long does it take?
₹60 e-Sevai service charge, typically 15–30 days.
