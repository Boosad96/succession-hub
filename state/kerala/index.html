Legal Heir Certificate in Kerala — Portal, Documents, Fee & Timeline

In Kerala the legal-heir certificate is issued by the Tahsildar / Revenue Department after a Village Officer enquiry, through e-District Kerala.

Issuing authorityVillage Officer / Tahsildar (Revenue Dept)
Portale-District Kerala
Fee₹2 stamp + ₹15 online (Akshaya centre ~₹28 total)
Timeline15–30 days (see note)

How to apply

Apply on e-District Kerala or at the Village Office / Akshaya centre. A Village Officer enquiry follows the application.

Fee

₹2 stamp + ₹15 online; about ₹28 in total at an Akshaya centre.

Timeline — two figures in the guides

Most guides say 15–30 days. IndiaFilings and Housing.com describe a 30-day gazette objection period plus 15 days to issue after it — about 45 days in total. Both figures are shown here because the sources disagree.

Documents usually asked for

  • Death certificate of the deceased (mandatory)
  • Identity proof of the applicant and all heirs
  • Address proof
  • Ration card / family register showing all family members
  • Proof of relationship to the deceased
  • Affidavit / joint affidavit from all heirs (where the state asks for one)

Frequently asked questions

Who issues the legal-heir certificate in Kerala?
The Tahsildar / Revenue Department, after a Village Officer enquiry.
How long does it take?
Most guides say 15–30 days. Some guides describe a 30-day objection period plus 15 days to issue (about 45 days).
What does it cost?
₹2 stamp + ₹15 online; about ₹28 in total at an Akshaya centre.
