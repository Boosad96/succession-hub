Legal-heir certificate — by state

A legal-heir certificate identifies who the legal heirs of a deceased person are. It is issued by the Tahsildar / Revenue Department — but not every state issues one. MaharashtraNo legal-heir certificate is issued in this state. Maharashtra has no notified 'legal-heir certificate' service. The equivalent is an heirship certificate under Bombay Regulation VIII of 1827, applied for in the civil court (City Civil Court / Bombay High Court original side, by value). The Maharashtra Court-Fees Act Sch.I Art.12 charges it at the probate rate on the property certified. Documents usually asked for

  • Death certificate of the deceased (mandatory)
  • Identity proof of the applicant and all heirs
  • Address proof
  • Ration card / family register showing all family members
  • Proof of relationship to the deceased
  • Affidavit / joint affidavit from all heirs (where the state asks for one)

Sources: Mookaa — Legal heir certificate documents, InvestorHelpdesk — Legal heir certificate guide, Bajaj Finserv — How to get a legal heir certificate

A legal-heir certificate is not a succession certificate. It identifies the heirs; it does not by itself authorise collecting bank deposits or shares — for that, banks usually ask for a succession certificate (or accept a nomination / indemnity, depending on the amount and the bank's rules).
